---
name: Pure webinar planning boundary
description: Why standard planning must remain separate from legacy scheduling and historical obligation deadlines.
---

Do not import the legacy webinar runtime's weekend-shifting policy into the pure standard planner, or wire the pure planner into existing runtime behavior without explicit authorization.

**Why:** The owner authorized a separate planning-only layer with local calendar-day recruitment offsets, not a migration of existing webinars or their persisted template policies. The older weekend convention and the new planning contract intentionally coexist.

**How to apply:** Consult canonical standards and current owner instructions before integration. This note is explanatory context, not an authoritative requirements source.

Keep historical obligation deadlines distinct from scheduled communication instants.

**Why:** Replanning must not erase an original registration or event-trigger deadline just because it is overdue. Conversely, preserving an overdue deadline is not permission to schedule a communication in the past or authorize sending.

**How to apply:** Retain descriptive deadlines with overdue status; require separate authorization and future-time validation for any later execution layer.
