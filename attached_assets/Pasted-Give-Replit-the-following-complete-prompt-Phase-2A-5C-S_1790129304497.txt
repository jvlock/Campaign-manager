7. Required scheduling semantics

The evaluators must preserve the already-approved scheduler behavior:

* 21 or more local calendar days: all four standard touches;
* 14–20 days: omit the 21-day touch, make the 14-day slot immediate, retain −7 and −1;
* 7–13 days: omit the first two, make the 7-day slot immediate, retain −1;
* 2–6 days: use the 7-day slot immediately and retain −1 only with at least 24 elapsed hours of separation;
* 0–1 days before start: only the final slot becomes immediate, with a compressed-window warning;
* started, completed, or cancelled events: all recruitment touches remain visible but omitted;
* immediate means exactly the supplied calculation instant;
* no scheduled communication may precede calculation;
* no scheduled communication may occur at or after webinar start;
* nominal past touches are omitted rather than backdated;
* local calendar offsets preserve webinar-local wall-clock time;
* DST gap and overlap handling remains explicit in metadata;
* omitted and adjusted touches never disappear.

Do not apply an older weekend-shifting policy to recruitment touches.
