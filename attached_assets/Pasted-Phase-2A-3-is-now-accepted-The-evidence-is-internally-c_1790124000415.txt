4. Authoritative time semantics

The webinar’s IANA time zone is authoritative.

The normal 21-, 14-, 7-, and 1-day offsets are local calendar-day offsets preserving the webinar’s local wall-clock time. They are not fixed multiples of 24 elapsed hours.

A daylight-saving transition may therefore produce a UTC difference of 23 or 25 hours for a one-calendar-day offset.

Use deterministic “compatible” disambiguation for calculated local times:

* nonexistent spring-forward time: move forward by the DST gap;
* repeated fall-back time: choose the earlier occurrence.

Expose any such adjustment in the result metadata. Do not perform it silently.

Before adding a new date-time dependency, inspect the repository for an existing capable library. Reuse it when suitable. If no existing implementation can safely handle IANA zones and DST, report the dependency proposed and why before adding it.

5. Shortened-window bands

Calculate local calendar days remaining from the calculation date to the webinar date in the webinar’s time zone.

Local calendar days remaining	Plan
21 or more	Schedule all four standard touches at −21, −14, −7, and −1 local calendar days
14–20	Omit the 21-day touch; adjust the 14-day touch to immediate; retain −7 and −1
7–13	Omit the 21- and 14-day touches; adjust the 7-day touch to immediate; retain −1
2–6	Omit the 21- and 14-day touches; adjust the 7-day touch to immediate; retain −1 only if it is at least 24 elapsed hours after the immediate touch
0–1, before start	Omit the first three touches; adjust the 1-day touch to immediate; show compressed-window warning
Event started, completed, or cancelled	Create no recruitment schedule; show every touch as omitted with the applicable reason

“Immediate” means exactly the supplied calculation instant.

For the 2–6-day band, the 24-hour separation test uses elapsed time, not local calendar-day difference.

After calculating the plan, enforce these invariants:

* no scheduled instant precedes the calculation instant;
* no communication is scheduled at or after webinar start;
* no duplicate communication identity;
* no duplicate scheduled instant;
* scheduled output is chronological;
* adjusted and omitted touches remain visible;
* no backdating.

If a nominal touch falls in the past because of the exact local time, omit it with a specific reason. Do not silently shift it unless the shortened-window table explicitly calls for an immediate adjustment.

7. Audience-state planner

Create a separate pure, immutable audience-state planning module.

Preserve structural separation:

* event status remains separate from participant status;
* attendance is recorded and evaluated per registrant;
* do not introduce a session-level attendance state.

Model these states distinctly:

* eligible non-registrant;
* registered;
* attended;
* registered but absent;
* attendance unknown;
* waitlisted;
* cancelled;
* internal or test.

The planner returns obligations, eligibility, suppressions, deadlines, and warnings. It does not send anything.

Required behavior

* Eligible non-registrants may receive only the calculated recruitment plan.
* Registration immediately suppresses all future recruitment.
* Registered participants receive:
    * immediate confirmation;
    * calendar and attendance information;
    * 24-hour reminder when still in the future;
    * 1-hour reminder when still in the future;
    * change or cancellation notice when triggered.
* Late registration never produces a reminder in the past.
* Attended participants receive only the attended follow-up variant.
* Registered-but-absent participants receive only the absent variant.
* Attendance unknown creates a reconciliation obligation first.
* Attendance unknown must never create both attended and absent variants.
* When still unresolved after two business days, attendance unknown may create only the approved neutral variant.
* Waitlisted participants receive waitlist confirmation and then promotion or closure obligations as triggered.
* Cancelled participants receive cancellation confirmation and suppression from later customer paths.
* Internal/test participants are excluded from customer communications and reporting.
* Explicitly labelled test-send planning may be represented for QA but must not be classified as a customer path.
* Shared follow-up assets are permitted, but attended, absent, and neutral communication variants remain distinct.
