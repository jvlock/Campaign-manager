## 2. Objective

Create one internal participant-lifecycle integration boundary:

```text
Synthetic registration or attendance event
              ↓
Participant lifecycle service
              ↓
Authoritative source-fact update
              ↓
Suppression and communication-obligation update
              ↓
Phase 2B-2 domain orchestration
              ↓
Immutable simulation snapshot
```

Requirements:

* Source facts must be persisted before evaluation.
* Suppression decisions must occur before any simulated provider handoff.
* Registration, participant status, attendance and event status remain distinct.
* Historical executed communications remain immutable and auditable.
* Future obligations may be suppressed or recalculated without rewriting history.
* Every resulting evaluation remains `simulation-only`.

## 4. Preserve separate state dimensions

The implementation must not collapse these concepts:

| Dimension            | Meaning                                                      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Event status         | State of the webinar occurrence                              |
| Registration status  | Whether a participant registered, cancelled or is waitlisted |
| Attendance status    | Attended, absent or unknown for that participant             |
| Communication status | Planned, scheduled, executed, cancelled or suppressed        |
| Suppression status   | Whether a future communication is prohibited and why         |

Rules:

* A completed event does not prove an individual attended.
* Registration does not prove attendance.
* Cancellation is not absence.
* Waitlist status is not registration confirmation.
* Email delivery, open or click is not attendance.
* Unknown attendance remains unknown.
* Event cancellation must not be represented as participant cancellation.
* Participant cancellation must not cancel the event occurrence.

Add tests for every distinction.

## 5. Synthetic participant boundary

Only synthetic participant data is authorized.

Create or reuse a development-only participant fixture mechanism that:

* generates synthetic stable participant identifiers;
* uses reserved non-deliverable email domains such as `.test`;
* prohibits real customer identifiers;
* prohibits imports and bulk uploads from production sources;
* rejects data that matches known production-source markers;
* clearly labels every record synthetic;
* records fixture provenance;
* supports deterministic test creation;
* can be safely removed with the disposable database.

Do not create a general open participant-data administration screen.

Any development interface must be explicitly labelled:

`Synthetic participant simulator — no customer data`

## 6. Registration integration

Implement an idempotent internal registration operation.

Required behavior:

1. Validate the occurrence.
2. Validate that the participant is synthetic.
3. Validate the requested transition.
4. Persist the registration source fact.
5. Immediately suppress future recruitment communications for that occurrence.
6. Recalculate future reminder obligations where applicable.
7. Preserve already-executed recruitment history.
8. Write the audit record in the same transaction.
9. Invoke the Phase 2B-2 orchestration service.
10. Persist the resulting immutable simulation snapshot.

Requirements:

* Duplicate registration events must not create duplicate participants, registrations, suppressions, audits or snapshots.
* Registration suppression must be effective before any simulated provider handoff.
* Suppressed recruitment communications remain visible with reason and timestamp.
* Registration must not suppress valid registrant reminders unless the canonical standard requires it.
* Registration changes only the relevant occurrence.
* Cross-occurrence suppression is prohibited unless explicitly required by a governed campaign exclusion.

## 7. Waitlist integration

Implement the canonical waitlist behavior using the existing model where possible.

Requirements:

* Waitlisting remains distinct from confirmed registration.
* Future recruitment treatment follows the canonical standard.
* Waitlisted participants must not receive communications reserved for confirmed registrants unless authorized.
* Promotion from waitlist to registration is atomic and idempotent.
* Promotion updates suppression and reminder obligations.
* A full or closed occurrence must not silently convert a waitlisted participant to registered.
* Historical waitlist status changes remain auditable.

Do not invent automated capacity-management rules if they are not already authorized.

## 8. Participant cancellation

Implement participant registration cancellation separately from event cancellation.

Requirements:

* Preserve the original registration record and cancellation history.
* Recalculate future reminders and follow-up obligations according to the canonical standard.
* Do not automatically return the person to recruitment.
* Do not assume cancellation means absence.
* Do not delete executed communications.
* Do not erase the original registration timestamp.
* Repeated cancellation requests must be idempotent.
* Any later re-registration must be an explicit valid transition with its own audit evidence.

If the canonical documentation does not settle a downstream communication path, fail closed and report the unresolved path rather than inventing behavior.

## 9. Event cancellation and rescheduling

Implement the internal effects of occurrence cancellation and rescheduling.

For cancellation:

* stop future recruitment and reminder obligations;
* preserve executed communications;
* preserve participant records and history;
* mark affected future communications suppressed or cancelled with explicit reasons;
* prevent attendance classification for an event that did not occur;
* trigger no external notifications in this increment.

For rescheduling:

* preserve the occurrence identity unless the existing authoritative model requires a new occurrence;
* recalculate future obligations using the canonical time-zone rules;
* preserve prior schedules and changes in audit history;
* identify communications that are now overdue, invalid or require review;
* do not silently move an already-executed communication;
* maintain idempotency.

Do not generate or send customer-facing cancellation or rescheduling messages.

## 10. Attendance integration

Implement a synthetic attendance import and reconciliation contract.

Every attendance record must be keyed to:

* participant;
* webinar activity;
* webinar occurrence;
* authoritative synthetic source event;
* source reference;
* observed timestamp;
* reconciliation timestamp;
* current attendance classification.

Requirements:

* Attendance remains per registrant.
* Aggregate event attendance cannot substitute for participant attendance.
* Duplicate source events are idempotent.
* Conflicting events remain visible and require deterministic reconciliation.
* Corrections create audit history rather than overwriting prior evidence silently.
* Attendance cannot be established from opens, clicks, page views or email delivery.
* A participant may remain attendance-unknown.
* Reconciliation must distinguish missing source data from an explicit absence result.
* Attendance changes invoke a new evaluation and immutable simulation snapshot.

Do not build a live ON24 import.

## 11. Follow-up variants

Map participant state to the canonical follow-up obligation:

* attended;
* absent;
* attendance unknown;
* cancelled or otherwise ineligible;
* internal/test exclusion.

Requirements:

* Neutral follow-up is distinct from attended and absent follow-up.
* Neutral follow-up may be selected only after the authorized attendance-reconciliation period.
* Development placeholder content cannot satisfy live content approval.
* Ineligible or cancelled participants must not be placed into a follow-up path merely because the event completed.
* Variant selection creates a plan or obligation, not proof of execution.
* No follow-up is sent in this increment.

## 12. Suppression service

Create or extend one authoritative suppression decision service.

It must evaluate, at minimum:

* confirmed registration;
* waitlist state;
* participant cancellation;
* event cancellation;
* internal/test exclusion;
* governed campaign exclusion when a valid observation exists;
* communication cancellation;
* previously executed communication;
* duplicate communication;
* occurrence mismatch;
* invalid participant state.

The suppression result must include:

* allowed or suppressed;
* exact reason code;
* human-readable explanation;
* affected participant;
* occurrence;
* communication;
* evaluated timestamp;
* source references;
* governed-observation provenance where applicable;
* release fingerprint;
* simulation-only status.

Requirements:

* Suppression is evaluated before provider handoff.
* No caller can bypass a non-bypassable exclusion.
* Manual free text cannot override suppression.
* Unknown or invalid states fail closed.
* Suppressed communications remain visible.
* Already-executed history remains unchanged.
* Repeated evaluation is deterministic.
* The service supports whole-population pagination without partial-result ambiguity.

Do not create a provider handoff in this phase.

## 13. Communication-obligation updates

When participant or event state changes, update only future communication obligations.

Requirements:

* Preserve historical planned and executed records.
* Record why a future obligation changed.
* Distinguish suppressed, cancelled, obsolete and rescheduled.
* Do not convert planned activity into execution evidence.
* Do not erase the original schedule.
* Maintain stable communication identifiers.
* Apply updates transactionally with the source-state change and audit event.
* Protect against concurrent registration, cancellation and attendance updates.
